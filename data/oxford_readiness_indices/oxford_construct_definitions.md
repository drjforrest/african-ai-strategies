# Oxford Government AI Readiness Index - construct definitions

Semantic reference text for every pillar, dimension and indicator, both framework eras.
The 2021-2024 framework is the comparable four-year panel; 2025 is the new six-pillar framework.

| dimension | Government | Vision |  | Whether the government has a clear vision for implementing artificial intelligence. | dimension | Government | Governance and Ethics |  | Whether the right regulations and ethical frameworks are in place to implement AI in a way that builds trust and legitimacy. ## 2025 framework

| Level | Pillar | Dimension | Indicator | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pillar | Policy Capacity |  |  | The extent to which the government can design and implement effective AI policies aligned with a clear, national policy vision for how AI benefits the country, demonstrating commitment by assigning resources to that vision and engaging in international cooperation to address AI's global nature. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report, p.6 |
| pillar | Governance |  |  | The extent to which the government has clear governance principles that shape how AI is developed and adopted while protecting people's rights and societal interests, promoted through its own public-sector practices and ensuring an adaptive environment for regulatory compliance with respect to AI. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report, p.6 |
| pillar | AI Infrastructure |  |  | The extent to which there is adequate compute capacity and enabling technical infrastructure that government and other actors can rely on over the long term to develop and use AI, with sufficient high-quality data that is accessible and flows securely within and across borders. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report, p.6 |
| pillar | Public Sector Adoption |  |  | The extent to which the government's digital policy encourages testing how AI can solve public-sector challenges and, where AI proves effective, scales those solutions, demonstrated through its e-government delivery capabilities. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report, p.6 |
| pillar | Development & Diffusion |  |  | The extent to which there is a mature AI sector able to meet the needs of local clients and consumers and push long-term AI innovation, backed by sufficient human capital to meet demand for wider AI technology diffusion among industry, researchers and civil society. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report, p.6 |
| pillar | Resilience |  |  | The extent to which the government manages the social, economic and environmental challenges of a societal transition towards widespread AI adoption, actively monitoring and preparing for new risks to national safety and security as AI development and diffusion progresses. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report, p.6 |
| dimension | Policy Capacity | Policy vision |  | Whether the government has published a clear national AI strategy and high-level vision, including an AI advisory council and stated intent to participate in international cooperation on AI research and governance. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | Policy Capacity | Policy commitment |  | Whether the strategy is backed by implementation: published actions, outputs and a timeline (a roadmap with designated ministries and monitoring methods), and updates to policy over time. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | AI Infrastructure | Compute capacity |  | Whether the country has adequate computational resources (for example data-centre and processing capacity) that government and other actors can rely on to develop and use AI. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | AI Infrastructure | Enabling technical infrastructure |  | Whether foundational connectivity and technical infrastructure (networks, broadband, cloud) are in place to support AI development and deployment. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | AI Infrastructure | Data quality |  | Whether sufficient, high-quality data is available and accessible for a wide range of AI problems, flowing securely within and across borders. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | Governance | Governance principles |  | Whether the government promotes clear principles for the responsible development and adoption of AI that protect people's rights and societal interests. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | Governance | Regulatory compliance |  | Whether an adaptive regulatory environment supports and enforces compliance for the development and use of AI. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | Public Sector Adoption | Government digital policy |  | Whether government digital policy encourages testing and piloting of AI to solve public-sector challenges. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | Public Sector Adoption | e-Government delivery |  | Whether the government scales effective AI solutions, demonstrated through the maturity of its digital and e-government service delivery. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | Development & Diffusion | Human capital |  | Whether there is sufficient human capital and skills to support a domestic AI sector and wider adoption of AI. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | Development & Diffusion | AI sector maturity |  | Whether there is a mature domestic AI sector able to meet the needs of local clients and drive long-term AI innovation. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | Development & Diffusion | AI technology diffusion |  | Whether AI technologies are diffusing among industry players, researchers and civil society who apply AI in their domains. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | Resilience | Societal transition |  | Whether the government manages the social, economic and environmental challenges of a societal transition towards widespread AI adoption. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| dimension | Resilience | Safety and security |  | Whether the government actively monitors and prepares for new risks to national safety and security as AI develops and diffuses. | Oxford Insights GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report |
| indicator | Policy Capacity | Policy vision | National AI Strategy | Evidence of an overarching published national AI strategy demonstrating a whole-of-government policy vision (100 published / 50 draft or announced / 0 none). | Desk research (GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report) |
| indicator | Policy Capacity | Policy vision | AI Advisory Council | A multistakeholder body of AI experts advising government on AI implementation and use, broader in remit than drafting the strategy itself. | Desk research (GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report) |
| indicator | Policy Capacity | Policy vision | Publication of Strategy Updates | Whether the country has published policy documentation beyond its original strategy (additional phase, implementation plan or further strategy). | Desk research (GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report) |
| indicator | Policy Capacity | Policy vision | International Cooperation on AI R&D | Active joint R&D collaborations, bilateral agreements, co-funded initiatives or hosting of major international AI R&D events. | Desk research (GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report) |
| indicator | Policy Capacity | Policy vision | International Cooperation on AI Governance | Engaged participation in global AI governance fora and contribution to international AI safety or governance standards. | Desk research (GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report) |
| indicator | Policy Capacity | Policy commitment | Actions, outputs and timeline in AI Strategy | Whether the strategy includes a robust implementation roadmap: specific actions, designated ministries, a timeline and monitoring methods (100 roadmap / 50 actions only / 0 none). | Desk research (GAIRI 2025 Methodology Report) |
